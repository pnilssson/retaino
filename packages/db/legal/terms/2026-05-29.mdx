Last updated: 2026-05-29

only for lawful customer reactivation, campaign preparation, SMS sending,
reply handling, reporting, billing, and related account administration.

## Customer responsibilities

The customer is responsible for account information, organization users,
imported contact data, campaign content, booking links, reported booking data,
and lawful use of Retaino.

The customer must:

- keep organization and billing details accurate;
- make sure users are authorized to act for the organization;
- remove users who should no longer have access;
- import only contact data that the customer may lawfully use in Retaino;
- keep marketing permissions and opt-out records current;
