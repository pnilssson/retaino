# Retaino SMS Marketing Attestation

Last updated: 2026-05-29

By accepting this attestation for an organization, the customer confirms the
following statements for SMS campaigns prepared or sent through Retaino.

## Attested statements

- Recipients are the customer's own valid contacts.
- The customer has valid consent or another valid legal basis for each
  recipient.
- Where the customer relies on an existing-customer exception for SMS marketing,
  the customer has met all required conditions, including own similar services,
  no opt-out, and a clear, simple, and free opt-out opportunity.
- Campaigns promote the customer's own services.
- No recipient has opted out.
- The customer will suppress recipients who opt out outside Retaino before
  sending further messages.
- Every campaign SMS includes a clear opt-out path.
- Imported data excludes health notes, journal notes, treatment notes,
  diagnoses, consultation notes, free-text medical fields, and other sensitive
  unsupported fields.
- Campaign content will not reveal or strongly imply sensitive health, medical,
  or treatment information about a recipient.
- Retaino may transmit recipient phone numbers, sender information, SMS content,
  delivery metadata, reply content, and opt-out event data to its SMS provider
  as needed to send campaigns, process replies, record delivery events, and
  maintain suppressions.

## Continuing obligation

The customer must keep these statements true for every import, audience,
campaign, test send, live send, manual reply, and message recipient. If the
statements stop being true for a contact, the customer must not send that
contact SMS through Retaino unless a later lawful basis is recorded.
