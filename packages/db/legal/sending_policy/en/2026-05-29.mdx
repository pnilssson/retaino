# Retaino Sending Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-29

This policy governs SMS marketing and related campaign activity in Retaino.

## Allowed use

Campaigns must promote the customer's own services to valid customer contacts.
The customer must have consent or another valid legal basis for each recipient
before importing contacts, preparing campaigns, sending test messages, sending
live campaigns, or replying manually.

For SMS marketing in Sweden, the customer must either have valid prior consent
or meet the conditions for the existing-customer exception. The existing-
customer exception generally requires that the customer received the contact
details in connection with a sale, the message concerns the customer's own
similar services, the recipient has not opted out, and the recipient was given a
clear, simple, and free opt-out opportunity when the contact details were
collected and in each later message.

## Customer permission obligations

The customer must:

- import only contacts who may lawfully receive marketing SMS from the
  organization;
- keep permission and opt-out status current;
- remove or suppress recipients who withdraw permission or opt out outside
  Retaino;
- use Retaino only for the customer's own services;
- make sure each campaign is honest, clear, and not misleading;
- make sure campaign content does not reveal sensitive information about a
  recipient.

## Provider and carrier rules

Campaigns must comply with Retaino policies, applicable law, Twilio messaging
requirements for the MVP SMS provider, telecommunications provider
requirements, and carrier filtering rules. Retaino may require sender
verification, numeric sender configuration, message content changes, opt-out
wording, or campaign changes before allowing a campaign to send.

## Prohibited use

The customer must not:

- send deceptive, unlawful, harassing, high-risk, or third-party promotional
  messages;
- use bought, scraped, rented, or otherwise unauthorized lists;
- message recipients who have opted out;
- import health notes, journal notes, treatment notes, diagnoses, consultation
  notes, free-text medical fields, or other sensitive unsupported fields;
- send campaign content that reveals or strongly implies sensitive health,
  medical, or treatment information about a recipient;
- use Retaino for emergency, regulated medical, political, adult, or other
  high-risk messaging unless Retaino has expressly approved that use in writing.

## Opt-out handling

Every campaign SMS must include a clear opt-out path. Retaino appends required
opt-out wording to campaign messages. The customer must honor opt-outs and must
not message recipients who have opted out unless a later lawful opt-in is
recorded.

Retaino may process inbound STOP-style replies through its SMS provider and add
the recipient to the customer's suppression list.

## Review and enforcement

Retaino may block imports, campaign preparation, test sends, live sends, or
manual replies when campaign data, recipient status, content, provider
requirements, carrier requirements, or account behavior appears to violate this
policy.
