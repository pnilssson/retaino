# Retaino Customer Privacy Notice Guide

Last updated: 2026-05-29

This guide helps customers understand what they may need to tell their own
clients before using Retaino. It is guidance, not legal advice. The customer is
responsible for its own privacy notice and marketing compliance.

## Why this matters

When a customer imports contacts into Retaino, the customer is normally the
controller for its client data. Retaino acts as processor under the Data
Processing Agreement.

Retaino's Privacy Policy explains how Retaino handles data for its own users and
operations. It does not replace the customer's privacy notice to the customer's
own clients.

## What customers should consider disclosing

Customers should consider whether their privacy notice explains:

- that the customer may use client contact details for SMS marketing,
  reactivation campaigns, appointment-related offers, replies, and follow-up;
- the legal basis used for SMS marketing, such as consent or an existing-
  customer exception where allowed;
- that Retaino helps the customer import contacts, prepare campaigns, send SMS,
  handle replies, process opt-outs, track booking links, and report bookings;
- that Twilio or another SMS provider may process phone numbers, sender
  numbers, message bodies, delivery metadata, replies, timestamps, and opt-out
  events;
- that Retaino may process click events when a recipient opens a campaign
  booking link;
- how recipients can opt out of marketing SMS;
- how long contact, campaign, SMS, reply, opt-out, click, and booking reporting
  records are kept;
- how clients can exercise privacy rights such as access, correction, deletion,
  restriction, portability, objection, and consent withdrawal;
- whether personal data may be processed outside the EU or EEA and what
  safeguards apply.

## Data minimization

Customers should not export or import more information than Retaino needs.
Retaino's MVP workflow needs contact and campaign data such as names, phone
numbers, email addresses, last booking dates, campaign membership, SMS activity,
clicks, opt-outs, replies, and reported booking outcomes.

Customers must not import health notes, journal notes, treatment notes,
diagnoses, consultation notes, free-text medical fields, or other sensitive
unsupported fields.

## SMS permission

Before importing contacts, customers should confirm that each contact may
lawfully receive marketing SMS from the organization. Customers should keep
permission and opt-out records current and must not send SMS to recipients who
have opted out unless a later lawful opt-in is recorded.
