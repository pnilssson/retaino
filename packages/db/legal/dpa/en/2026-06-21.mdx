# Retaino Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: 2026-06-21

This Data Processing Agreement applies when Retaino processes personal data for
the customer as processor. The customer is the controller for imported contacts,
campaign data, SMS activity, booking references, reported outcomes, and related
customer-controlled data.

## Processing instructions

Retaino processes personal data only on documented customer instructions,
including instructions expressed through product configuration, account
settings, imports, campaign preparation, campaign sends, replies, reported
bookings, support requests, and this agreement.

Retaino will inform the customer if Retaino believes an instruction infringes
applicable data protection law, unless the law prevents Retaino from doing so.

## Processing scope

The subject matter is customer reactivation, SMS campaign delivery, two-way SMS
reply handling, opt-out handling, tracking, billing support, diagnostics,
security, and related reporting.

The duration is the customer's subscription period plus the period needed for
deletion, return, legal retention, security, backup, billing, dispute, or audit
purposes.

Retaino may process the categories of data, data subjects, and operations listed
in the processing schedule below.

## Customer obligations

The customer must:

- have a lawful basis for the personal data and marketing activity;
- provide required privacy information to its own clients;
- keep marketing permissions and opt-outs current;
- avoid uploading health notes, journal notes, treatment notes, diagnoses,
  consultation notes, and other sensitive unsupported fields;
- use Retaino only for lawful messages about the customer's own similar
  services;
- respond to data subject requests and regulator requests as controller.

## Retaino personnel

Retaino will ensure that persons authorized to process personal data are bound
by confidentiality obligations or are under an appropriate statutory obligation
of confidentiality.

## Security

Retaino will maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures designed
to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration,
disclosure, or destruction.

Current measures include:

- authenticated access to the app;
- organization-level access controls;
- tenant-scoped database queries;
- environment separation between local development and production;
- encryption in transit where supported by providers;
- provider credential protection through environment bindings and secrets;
- webhook signature validation for supported provider callbacks;
- suppression checks before campaign sends and manual replies;
- audit records for legal acceptance, imports, campaign decisions, and other
  user-driven administrative actions;
- filtering of sensitive fields from backend and web app Sentry error events
  where Retaino controls event payloads;
- temporary raw import file storage with deletion after processing and
  lifecycle cleanup fallback.

## Subprocessors

The customer authorizes Retaino to use subprocessors listed in the Subprocessor
Notice. Retaino will bind subprocessors by written obligations that protect
customer personal data to a standard materially consistent with this agreement.

Retaino will provide notice of material subprocessor changes through the
product, email, or published legal pages. The customer may object on reasonable
data protection grounds before the change takes effect. If Retaino cannot
reasonably resolve the objection, either party may terminate the affected
service.

## International transfers

Retaino will use an appropriate transfer mechanism for restricted transfers,
such as an adequacy decision, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU-US
Data Privacy Framework where applicable, or another lawful mechanism available
under applicable data protection law. Transfer details are described in the
Transfer Terms.

## Assistance

Taking into account the nature of processing and information available to
Retaino, Retaino will provide reasonable assistance for:

- data subject access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability,
  objection, and consent withdrawal requests;
- security incident investigation and notification duties;
- data protection impact assessments and prior consultation where required;
- regulator requests where the customer cannot reasonably handle the request
  without Retaino.

## Personal data breaches

Retaino will notify the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a
personal data breach affecting customer personal data processed by Retaino.
Retaino will provide available information reasonably needed for the customer to
meet its own notification duties.

## Deletion and return

Upon termination or written request, Retaino will delete or return customer
personal data unless Retaino must keep it for legal, security, billing, dispute,
backup, or audit reasons. Retaino may delete data according to the product
settings and retention rules.

## Audits

Retaino will make information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance
available to the customer. Retaino may satisfy audit requests through
documentation, security summaries, written responses, and commercially
reasonable cooperation. Audits must be reasonable in scope, respect security and
confidentiality obligations, and avoid disrupting Retaino operations.
