# Retaino Transfer Terms

Last updated: 2026-05-29

These terms apply when Retaino or a Retaino subprocessor processes personal data
outside the European Union or European Economic Area, or allows access to
personal data from outside the EU or EEA.

## Transfer mechanisms

Retaino will use an appropriate transfer mechanism where required, such as:

- an adequacy decision;
- the EU Standard Contractual Clauses;
- the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable;
- binding corporate rules where applicable;
- another lawful mechanism available under applicable data protection law.

Retaino will assess transfer risks relevant to the service, data categories,
destination, vendor role, and available safeguards.

## Current transfer context

Retaino uses providers that may process or access personal data from locations
outside the EU or EEA. This includes providers used for authentication, hosting,
database infrastructure, API infrastructure, object storage, payments,
background jobs, SMS delivery, diagnostics, and error monitoring.

Current providers include Clerk, Neon, Vercel, Cloudflare, Stripe, Inngest,
Twilio, and Sentry. The applicable transfer mechanism depends on the provider,
service configuration, processing location, support access, and current vendor
data protection terms.

## Twilio SMS processing

Twilio is the MVP SMS provider for campaign sends, test sends, delivery status
callbacks, inbound replies, sender provisioning, and opt-out handling. Twilio
may process recipient phone numbers, sender numbers, SMS message bodies,
delivery metadata, reply content, provider message identifiers, timestamps, and
opt-out events as needed to provide messaging services.

Where configured, Retaino may use Twilio EU SMS data residency features so
certain SMS personal data is processed and stored in Twilio's Ireland region
until it reaches telecommunications provider connections. SMS routing, carrier
delivery, telecommunications provider processing, redundancy, support access,
and security operations may still involve processing or access outside the EU or
EEA under the relevant transfer mechanisms and safeguards.

## Supplementary safeguards

Retaino will use reasonable supplementary safeguards where appropriate,
including:

- access controls;
- encryption in transit where supported;
- vendor due diligence;
- contractual limits on processing;
- security and incident response obligations;
- data minimization;
- filtering of sensitive diagnostic data where Retaino controls the payload.

## Customer cooperation

The customer must provide accurate information about its intended processing and
must not import data that Retaino has prohibited or cannot lawfully process
under the agreed safeguards.
