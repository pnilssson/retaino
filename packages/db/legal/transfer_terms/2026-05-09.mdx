# Retaino Transfer Terms

These terms apply when a Retaino vendor or subprocessor processes personal data
outside the European Union or European Economic Area.

## Transfer mechanism

Retaino will use an appropriate transfer mechanism, such as the European
Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, an adequacy decision, binding corporate
rules where applicable, or another lawful mechanism available under applicable
data protection law.

## Twilio SMS processing

Twilio is the MVP SMS provider for campaign sends, delivery status callbacks,
inbound replies, and opt-out handling. Twilio may process recipient phone
numbers, SMS message bodies, delivery metadata, reply content, and opt-out
events as needed to provide messaging services.

Where configured, Retaino may use Twilio's EU SMS data residency features so SMS
personal data is processed and stored in Twilio's Ireland region until it reaches
Twilio's telecommunications provider connections. SMS routing, carrier delivery,
telecommunications provider processing, redundancy, and support access may still
involve processing or access outside the EU or EEA under the relevant transfer
mechanisms and safeguards.

## Transfer assessment

Retaino will assess transfer risks that are relevant to the service, the data
categories, the destination, and the safeguards available from the vendor.

## Supplementary safeguards

Retaino will use reasonable supplementary safeguards where appropriate, including
access controls, encryption in transit, vendor due diligence, contractual limits,
and incident response obligations.

## Customer cooperation

The customer must provide accurate information about its intended processing and
must not import data that Retaino has prohibited or cannot lawfully process under
the agreed safeguards.
